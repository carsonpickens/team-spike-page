What it does

Everything a practice needs

Built with a Massachusetts high school team and used at practice every day. One app for the four jobs every running coach does by hand.

🎙️

Voice roll call

Hold the button and read names off. Team Spike checks athletes off as you say them, handles nicknames and "Last, First", and flags anyone it isn't sure about. Speech is recognized on the iPhone and never sent anywhere.

⏱️

Workouts with everyone's paces

Build the session once: warm-up, main set, cool-down. Every athlete gets their own threshold, interval and rep paces from their race times, in a grid by training group.

📅

Plan the week

Lay out the week around your meets, with a suggested shape for hard and easy days. Share it with athletes and parents on a public page, no accounts needed.

🏁

Results and PRs

Finds your meets' results on Athletic.net, or reads a photo or PDF of the results sheet. PRs, fitness scores and leaderboards by event, kept up to date for you.

🔭

Scout the next meet

Who's racing, their best runners, and your projected score before the gun. A projected finish, athlete by athlete, and who each of your runners should key off.

👥

Groups and live timing

Suggested training groups from real marks. Time reps live, or hand an assistant a link to time their group from their own phone.

Made for minors' data

Private by design

Only your coachesAthlete data is visible only to the coaches of that team. Every table is locked down row by row.
Voice stays on the phoneAudio and transcripts are never saved, logged or uploaded.
No photos of athletesTeam Spike never captures faces or images of athletes.
Public page on your termsThe shared schedule shows first names and last initials, or no names. Turn it off anytime.

Get Team Spike on your iPhone

The beta is free and runs through Apple's TestFlight app.

  1. Enter your name and the email you use for your Apple Account.
  2. We look over each request. Once it's approved, Apple emails you an invitation from TestFlight (new builds are also checked by Apple before they go out).
  3. Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invite on your iPhone, and tap Install.
  4. Sign in with Apple and set up your team in a couple of minutes, or try the demo team first.

We use your email only to send the TestFlight invite, through Apple. The app is for iPhone (iOS 17 or later).
