Team Spike privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Team Spike is an iPhone app for high school cross country and track coaches. This policy says what it collects, why, and who it shares it with. Athletes' information is minors' information, and the app is built around protecting it.

Who uses the app

Team Spike is for coaches. Athletes do not sign in to this app. A coach enters their team's roster and records attendance, workouts and results.

What we collect

  • Your account: the name and email address from Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google.
  • Your team: the school, the roster (athletes' names, graduation year, gender and events), attendance, training groups, workouts, meet schedules and results that you enter or ask the app to find.
  • Location, once: if you choose to find your school on a map during setup, your location is used once to search for nearby schools. It is not saved.

What we don't collect

  • Audio and voice: voice roll call is transcribed on your iPhone only. Audio and transcripts are never saved, logged, uploaded or used for analytics.
  • Photos of athletes: the app never captures faces or images of athletes.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of any data.

Who can see your team's data

Only the coaches of a team can see its athletes' information. This is enforced on the server for every table, not just in the app. If you turn on the public schedule page, anyone with that link sees the week's workouts and meets, and athletes' first names and last initials only if you allow names. You can hide names, change the link or turn the page off at any time.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores your account and team data.
  • Anthropic processes results sheets you upload, your school's name, your roster's names and public meet results to read results and scouting information. Files you upload are not stored; only the marks read from them are saved.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and TestFlight. Google provides Sign in with Google.
  • Athletic.net and other public results sites are read from your iPhone when you ask the app to find results or scout a meet.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Team → Delete my account), which deletes your login and any team you coach alone, with its athletes and records.

Beta sign-ups

If you ask for the beta on our website, we keep your name, email, role and school to send the TestFlight invitation through Apple, and use them for nothing else.
